This is the starting prompt for this video game:
**Game idea: Coyote Pack**
A hilly urban neighborhood has been settled by a gray urban coyote.  This brave settler has come to the area by crossing a bridge in the game's intro (credits/start screen and maybe a click-through-able short cinematic on first play).  The coyote sees the bridge now go up, and they are now in a neighborhood not unlike Queen Anne in Seattle:  The area has parks and also hillside woods thick with blackberry bushes and pine trees.  The area also has houses on blocks and people and all the stuff they come with: cars and buses and scooters and bikes; you can mostly ignore these NPCs as they are more afraid of you than you are of them.  Be careful, though--every once in a while the police might show up--they are pretty bad at corralling you, but be careful: and if you cause enough disturbance in this quiet neighborhood, the Feds might come after you and shoot at your from helicopters.  But that world be after causing trouble for a very long extended period--they are very busy doing that same kind of work elsewhere.
Over time as you find food sources (hunting critters, chasing NPCs carrying groceries until they drop them,  or rooting through trash bins) you get stronger and more experienced.  Eventually you may run into other coyotes--learn the right buttons to press as you try to make friends, and even form a roving pack (gang) with them.  This allows you to take over entire events like the popular farmer's market--as a lone coyote you would be unlikely to scare away the whole crowd and you might be met with some pushback, whereas you can overwhelm even an event with cops if you have an estimable pack (up to four coyotes; they are 'ad hoc' and last for a semi-randomized semi-event-driven period of several minutes; the more success in finding food and evading cops, the better the pack cohesion and easier it will be to later reassemble that pack).
Crows play a role as well as they are sometimes willing to horse-trade with the coyotes as they claim that the coyotes are fellow wild animals.  So the crows will offer the ability to quickly surveil far-off areas and report back--and they have a wide network so they will sometimes offer updates about where worthwhile events like picnics and Farmer's Markets will be happening.
The game should be a web app, probably in JS (etc.), be for the web desktop and also preferably the mobile web; the game screen should have built-in controls, where on the left will be a touch pad for directional control, and on the right a set of four buttons, gaming controller style, with each button having a light purple psychedelic gradient. The left button should be the paws emoji (this is the button to interact/socialize--much easier with wolves but possible with crows and some humans); next clockwise button should be the emoji that looks like a wolf--this one is for acting like a wild canine--hunt if there is a rabbit, growl if a wolf is nearby, or descend on a dropped grocery bag and rip it apart for its delicious food.
The next button should be for consuming--drink if near a stream or puddle, eat if near a ripped grocery bag, killed prey, etc.  The fourth is a select or confirm type of button.
The neighborhood/town should be called Lavender Hill and the style of the area is fairly traditional old houses mixed in with square modernist rebuilds; there is a main commercial strip with the grocery stores and coffee shops and then several non-playable businesses (frozen yogurt place, some restaurants, hardware store, clinic, etc.) and the coyote player should be able to enter the grocery stores intermittently when the automatic doors open and then can pretty easily scavenge food for a while, usually police response time is pretty slow.  The Farmer's Market should be every once in a while on a street intersecting with the main commercial strip and be another food source.  The coffee shops should be full of coffee which you can drink (but it doesn't help you much and is more of an intoxicant because you are a coyote) and the NPCs run and scream more than usual in coffee shops. 
The game's visual style should be in a fairly modern gaming style but can be simple in terms of graphics.  The working directory should have a folder called ['art'](./art/) and that will contain several artworks that should inform the style of the game.
One other animals in the game should be the Canada goose, possums, hare rabbits, outdoor cats, and foxes.  The first 3 can be hunted and the last 2 can socialize and even form parts of packs every once in a while.  The rare hunting target is the deer, which can usually only be hunted effectively in a pack of at least 2.
NPCs will sometimes walk with dogs--if you confront them, the dog may run or engage with you.
Should be a pause button, probably placed somewhere subtle near the right side controller buttons.  Should be a Settings button somewhere, maybe upper left. When paused, a screenshot (the screen when paused) button should be available; taking one shows it, and a download button; screenshot should have the credit "Coyote Pack by Daniel McKeown https://altaredwood.work" and a timestamp somewhere on the upper or lower edge of the image.  Settings page should have tabs for info game stats, Controls (explanation of game controls), Account (can create or log in/log out and also delete account if logged in, a Save tab where the current progress can be saved, Debug tab showing info like what type of data persistence is currently running (app backend should be PHP and use a text based data persistence story, SQLite or something like it; and have a conf file with optional configuration for mySQL/MariaDB.), a tab for Credits with the same credits as above ("Coyote Pack by [Daniel McKeown](https://altaredwood.work")), a tab for Terms with a terms of service for the game, and a tab for Privacy with a realistic privacy policy for the game if it were deployed as a web site (or locally).
Out on the waters edge you might run into sea lions.  They are occasionally up for a little conversation, but don't start anything with them--even a whole pack can do nothing to even one of these monsters.
The animals that you have conversations with will say stuff in a dialogue modal; if the config file
is set up for it (model name, API url, whatever is needed) [probably should default to using a local Ollama server; should ideally also be able to be optionally configured to use APIs of popular LLM cloud providers--this could allow API features when deployed] then the app will use an AI API to craft the animal's responses to the player (falling back to defaults as AI API may not be configured in many situations).
The game's logo should be an image of a gray coyote in a bold purple bowler hat, somewhat 'flat design' but with a little detail.
The game should feature rain intermittently, about 1/5 of the total time.  Day and night cycles should happen, with a total day lasting 3 minutes and the dark and light varying seasonally.
The game can have a slightly sketched, surreal quality in terms of appearance.  It should have somewhat 3D objects and show the game from a slightly set back 3rd person perspective.  Humans are mostly just NPCs to chase but there is one man in town who is a friendly wizard you can communicate with him and he gives you treats and there is a friendly witch who you can talk to and offers you magic (one time spells you can use to fly around for about ten seconds).  And there is also a jack-o-lantern monster with legs that walks around and scares humans but you can talk to.
Colors in this game should be diverse but be concentrated heavily on Earth tones.  Moon should have cycles and be visible sometimes at night.
If possible the game should have a 'soundtrack' made up of calm ambient sounds, very slowly and slightly randomly played--not discordant, just chill and soothing.  One of the buttons should allow a coyote to make a coyote bark (at least in some situations) and they should be able to do a group howl after a successful pack).  There should be a mute button next to the pause button.
Coyote should be able to sleep (lasts for equivalent of 5 hours; time moves faster during this period); when sleeping in wooded areas, will be left alone; may have curious NPCs come up to you and interrupt sleep when sleeping in busy areas especially during the day.
